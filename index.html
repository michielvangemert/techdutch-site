Roadmap

From strategy to execution, in six phases.

One roadmap, from your growth plan to people in seats. Start at phase one, or step in where you are today. Open roles can't wait, so quick wins run alongside from week one.

STRATEGY EXECUTION
01STRATEGY

Discover

Where is the business going, and what does that mean for people?

  • Growth plan and business goals
  • Leadership workshop, on site or remote
  • Audit of current hiring: sources, funnel, drop-off

You get: a clear baseline and shared priorities

02STRATEGY

Define

Which roles, when, where — and how to fill each one.

  • Workforce plan for 12–36 months
  • Critical roles and priorities
  • Build, buy or borrow per role, per country

You get: a talent strategy and hiring roadmap

03DESIGN

Design

Why should the right people choose you — and what happens when they do?

  • Employer value proposition
  • Candidate journey and interview process
  • Role profiles, salary bands, tooling and ATS

You get: a hiring blueprint your team can follow

04EXECUTION

Build

Put the engine in place before you need it.

  • Careers page and LinkedIn presence
  • Sourcing playbooks and outreach templates
  • Talent pools per role family and market

You get: a visible brand and a warm pipeline

05EXECUTION

Execute

Fill the seats, with a fractional recruiter in your team.

  • Active sourcing and discreet outreach
  • Screening and ready-to-interview shortlists
  • Interview coordination through to offer

You get: the right people, hired

06EXECUTION

Measure & improve

Know what works, and do more of it.

  • Time to hire, source quality, offer acceptance
  • Retention of new hires after 6 and 12 months
  • Quarterly review and plan update

You get: a hiring machine that keeps getting better
